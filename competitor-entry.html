Privacy Notice & Entry Information

Privacy Act 2020

Waimarino Shears Incorporated operates this online entry system for the competition organiser. Personal information submitted through this form is handled in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and the Information Privacy Principles.

Collecting and holding agency: Waimarino Shears Incorporated, 11 Duncan Street, Raetihi 4632, New Zealand. The competition organiser receives and uses the entry information for the competition purposes described below.

What information is collected

The entry record may contain your name, hometown, grade or event, mobile number, email address, entry reference, submission time, and later check-in or paid/cleared status recorded by competition entry staff.

Your competitor name and grade/event are required to enter. Hometown is optional. You must provide at least one contact method — mobile number or email address — so entry staff can contact you about your entry. If the required information is not provided, the online entry cannot be accepted.

Why it is collected and who may use it

The information is collected for competition entry, contacting you about your entry or attendance, competition administration, check-in, entry-fee management, draw preparation, timing-system setup and operation, competition records, and technical or data queries relating to the event.

Your entry information may be accessed by the competition organiser, authorised competition entry or timing staff, and Waimarino Shears as the system operator. Contact details are not included in the timing-system roster or public results.

Data life cycle

  1. Your information is collected when you submit this form.
  2. It is stored in the competition's Entry Manager record.
  3. It is used for the competition purposes listed above and for directly related purposes.
  4. It is protected against unauthorised access, use, change, loss or disclosure.
  5. Personal information is kept only for as long as it is needed for a lawful competition, record-keeping, technical or administrative purpose, then removed or anonymised when it is no longer required.

Access or correction

You may ask for access to personal information held about you, or ask for it to be corrected. Privacy requests can be sent to Waimarinoshears@gmail.com. Entry-specific questions should normally go to the competition organiser listed below.

Entry, payment and check-in

Submitting this form means your entry has been received. On competition day, you must check in with the competition entry staff. If your entry fee has not already been paid or cleared, you will need to pay it at check-in unless the organiser has told you otherwise.

Your name will only be included in the draw for your grade or event once entry staff have recorded you as checked in and paid/cleared.

Automatic entry receipt

If you provide an email address, an automatic receipt will be sent from the Waimarino Shears entry system after your entry is accepted. The email will identify the competition and give you the organiser's contact details. SMS confirmation is not currently sent.
